Detailed Description

The Current Time characteristic (0x2A2B): 10 bytes with the date and time (Exact Time 256), the day of week, fractions of a second and the reason for the last adjustment.

Use fromUnixMs() and toUnixMs() to convert from and to the time in milliseconds since 1970-01-01. The conversion does not use the time zone: it is up to the user of the library if the date and time are UTC (default) or the local time (see TinyCTSServer::setTimeIsLocal()).
